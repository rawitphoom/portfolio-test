About Me

I’m Rawi. A UX/UI designer and front-end developer in Burnaby, BC. I just finished my B.Sc. in Interactive Arts and Technology at SFU. I design things and then I build them myself. Handing a file off and walking away has never really worked for me. Most recently that was LintelAI’s marketing site, start to finish, on my own, with the same few albums on repeat.

I like the messy middle of a project . Working out what the real problem is before anyone has agreed on what to build . Then designing it. Then building it . Then fixing the parts that only break once it is real .

A site is never one thing . Layout. Type. Motion . Copy. The details nobody notices until they are wrong.

So say something . The phone below actually answers.
